Forever Rogue

The do-everything Rogue companion for World of Warcraft: Forever. For every Rogue race and both factions. Type /frogue.

What it does

  • Pick Pocket log and earnings. Every pickpocket: coin, items, junkbox tier, mob, level and zone (inside dungeons the game hides mob names, so it says "unknown mob"). Session, day and lifetime totals, copper per pickpocket, pickpockets per hour, and your best mobs. A pocket refill timer per mob.
  • Best targets. Pickpocket targets by level and zone, including community data from GoldScout players, shown with sample sizes and marked unconfirmed, next to your own log.
  • Junkboxes and Lockpicking. Boxes in your bags (and bank), whether you can open them, what they're worth (Auctionator prices if you have it, vendor prices if not), the lockpicking route, and locked dungeon doors.
  • Poisons. What's on each weapon (a poison and a sharpening stone can share a weapon in Forever), stock in bags, and which poison each hand should carry.
  • Rogue bar. A Pick Lock button, main-hand and off-hand poison buttons, reagent counts and a pocket counter. One press = one action, set up out of combat, no keys bound by default.
  • Rotation help for Assassination, Combat and Subtlety (advice only), talents and builds, gear tips with weapon-speed notes, bag upgrades, leveling, class quests for your race, gold, PvP basics, and Learn the Rogue.

Tested on Rip

Rip is a Human Rogue of the Alliance in Northshire Valley, and the test character for this addon. Screenshots and art come later.

Install

On Linux with OmaForge: omaforge install -c forever github:ninepointlabs/forever-rogue. Anywhere else: download the zip from GitHub Releases and unpack ForeverRogue into _forever_/Interface/AddOns. Then /reload and /frogue.
